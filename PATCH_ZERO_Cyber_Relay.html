Worth going over again

    How to play

    Fifteen safe houses have gone dark. Each one radios in with a situation. Choose the call you'd actually make, then lock it in.

    Generator charge

    A correct first call feeds 100 charge into the tower. Asking Vex for help costs 40 before you answer. A wrong call earns nothing.

    Horde pressure

    Every wrong call raises pressure by 25%. Hit 100% and the fence comes down — you lose 150 charge and the night gets harder. Pressure eases slightly with each clean call.

    Keyboard

    Press A, B, C or D (or 1–4) to pick an option. Press Enter to lock it in, and Enter again to move to the next call.

    One thing worth knowing

    Every scenario here is a real thing that happens to real people. The zombies are set dressing. The advice isn't.

    Field guide

    Where to go when something happens for real, in Australia.

    • Scamwatch (run by the National Anti-Scam Centre) — scamwatch.gov.au
    • Scam texts can be forwarded to 7226 (that spells SCAM) free of charge.

    Hacking, ransomware and stolen accounts

    • ReportCyber — cyber.gov.au, run by the Australian Signals Directorate.
    • IDCARE for identity theft support — idcare.org

    Bullying, image abuse and harmful content

    • eSafety Commissioner — esafety.gov.au
    • Kids Helpline, 24/7, 1800 55 1800 — kidshelpline.com.au

    Checking if your details leaked

    • Have I Been Pwned — haveibeenpwned.com
